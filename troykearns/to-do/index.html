Why this matters

In my analysis, the comment section almost NEVER seems to know who you are personally, whether a video is popular or not. You don’t yet have a strong community that knows you. We need to build a STRONG, healthy community—and that starts with your face as your profile picture so newcomers know you are Troy, the person in the video.

Use a clear, recognizable headshot that is easy to see at profile-picture size.

Help people realize you are the guy in the video. A comment in your own voice, alongside your headshot, connects your account to the person they just watched.

Example: “Troy here—ask me anything about what we did in this video.” Keep it natural to each video, and follow through by replying.

Use YouTube’s Related Video feature to give each relevant Short a direct path to the full story. Add it when publishing and revisit existing Shorts that have a relevant long-form video.
