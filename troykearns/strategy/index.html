Strategy / Short-form content

Content is king.

Interesting people, genuinely engaged in the process. That is the clearest difference I see between Troy’s strongest videos and the recent content that has struggled.

This is my qualitative assessment of short-form content. Long-form analysis is still to come.

The finding

Give people a reason to care.

What stands out in the strongest videos

People with personality who are truly involved in what is happening. Their questions, decisions and reactions give the audience something worth watching and sharing.

What needs to change

My concern with ad-first content is that the promotion becomes the reason for the video. The audience needs a worthwhile story, useful insight or real interaction before we ask anything of them.

Three forms to lean into

Different formats. The same standard.

01

In the field

Put real people and real work at the center. Let Troy’s involvement, the decisions being made and people’s honest reactions carry the video.

02

Talking head & podcast

Lead with a clear idea or an engaged conversation. Give the audience insight they can use, with Troy’s personality and judgment coming through naturally.

03

Choreographed & scripted

Keep the planning; change the emphasis. Build a worthwhile piece of content, then let the book or call to action become a natural part of it.

The content should still be worth watching if the promotion were removed.

The creative direction

Make trust visible.

Present Troy in an authentic, shareable way. Let his actions reveal good character, prudence and sober-minded judgment. Show the behavior that earns trust, with room for real people to be themselves.

  • Are the people genuinely engaged?
  • Does the audience get something worthwhile?
  • Does Troy’s judgment come through in what he does or says?
  • Would someone share this for the content itself?
