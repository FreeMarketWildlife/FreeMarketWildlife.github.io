Strategy / Short-form content

Content is king.

Interesting people, genuinely engaged in the process. That is the clearest difference I see between Troy’s strongest videos and the recent content that has struggled.

This is my qualitative assessment of short-form content. Long-form analysis is still to come.

The finding

Give people a reason to care.

This is What Works

  • Interesting characters who genuinely care about what is happening.
  • Real conversations, honest reactions and natural personality.
  • People actively involved in the work and decisions.
  • Troy participating in the process and sharing his judgment.
  • Useful insight that gives people a reason to watch and share.

Why Your Most Recent Shorts SUCK

  • The pitch takes over the video.
  • Scripted delivery feels performed instead of authentic.
  • Too little genuine interaction with interesting people.
  • The book or offer overshadows the actual content.
  • Asking for action before giving viewers a reason to care.
  • Less personality, real involvement and reason to share.

The creative direction

Make trust visible.

  • Show sound judgment. Explain decisions and tradeoffs.
  • Treat people with dignity. Let them speak and take their concerns seriously.
  • Be honest about setbacks. Share mistakes and what they taught you.
  • Stay sober-minded. Be calm and thoughtful when situations get difficult.
  • Show the work. Let actions and follow-through establish credibility.
  • Be useful first. Share experience without making every moment a sales pitch.
  • Keep it authentic. Let Troy’s character come through in real interactions.
