# Forest interaction criteria

This is a design and acceptance target, not a statement that every route passes. The user's roughly one-second encounter goal concerns the playable forest bounce loop. It must not be achieved by invisible fixtures, arbitrarily enlarged animals, taller trees, endless micro-bounces or repeated camera/time manipulation.

## Where the cadence applies

- The authored forest extends from the oak clearing through x733m. Its low floor route is0–8m above the floor; ordinary trunks/canopies are30–60m tall. Four rare emergents reach85–88.5m. The fantasy oak remains110m, with its broad crown around x−8m.
- For a conservative repeatable audit, the **forest play band** is x−46…733m and altitude0…110m. It includes the aerial travel between crowns: being above an ordinary35m tree is not a reason to dismiss an empty50m-high route. Contacts there need either an honest supported surface or an existing proportionate aerial interactor.
- Above110m is intentional open sky, followed by the authored starlight band at960m. The one-second collision target does not apply to these sustained ascents. Gravity is still present until the actual3,000m escape state. Re-entering the forest band starts a new measured interaction segment; long forest descent gaps still count.

## What counts

Use the actual released body and its complete sequenced contact events. Record release-to-first, inter-contact and last-to-finish gaps. Report raw whole-flight gaps alongside the portions continuously inside the forest play band. Sampled boundary crossings carry their stated time-resolution uncertainty. Never call a capped/truncated contact history complete.

Physical impact events already reject resting support and very small seam taps. Foliage/sap resistance fields are recorded separately and do not stand in for a landing. Existing encounter powers count only after actual contact with their visible canonical body. Repeating a contact against the same fixture is not the same as reaching another authored destination: report distinct surfaces and existing encounter types as well.

The target is roughly no more than one second without a useful interaction in an intentional forest chain. Keep the longest gap and distribution visible; a good mean or higher object count cannot hide a several-second empty stretch. Weak launches may deliberately stop on the home branch and are tested for safe completion rather than an invented minimum distance or bounce count.

## Gates for this bounded correction

1. Use one real finite-force Enter carry, observed support, powered winding and actual pin release. No substituted loading pose, launch velocity, support flags, collision immunity or personal save is allowed.
2. Compare the released baseline and candidate at minimum/75%/full draw on starter, Medium, Bear and Elephant, plus75%/full with each existing Slingshot, Staff and Air intervention. Record the exact deterministic controller; this is36 discrete cases, not continuous or human-play certification.
3. Every case must launch and finish without new pre-release obstruction, numerical/material failure or pathological aftermath. Preserve existing geometry, masses, species and encounter radii. No longer forest or whole-flight gap over a0.05s comparison tolerance is accepted merely because another route improves.
4. A targeted route must gain a useful visible encounter or shorten its actual forest gap. Reject near-halved progress, prolonged low-energy aftermath or unreadable tiny surprises as a price for better gap numbers. A different range alone is not a universal improvement; retain and review the measured tradeoff.
5. Check encounter size against actual camera scale on desktop/phone. A30cm bird can be readable beside a mouse/rabbit and nearly subpixel beside an Elephant; do not use the latter as an invisible large-animal bumper. Render/controls/camera comfort require actual supported browser acceptance, which remains pending when that capability is unavailable.
6. Preserve custom maps. Only exact untouched prior defaults migrate in memory; startup does not write stored bytes. Authoring, save/load/export and Play use the same canonical entities and physics.

Passing these bounded non-regression gates permits a tested checkpoint. It does **not** certify the one-second forest target, novice comprehension, native camera comfort or every possible player trajectory. Report remaining failed targets and intentional open-sky time explicitly.
