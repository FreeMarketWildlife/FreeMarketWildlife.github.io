How to play

WASD or arrows move; Shift sprints; Space slashes; P pauses. Enter a local name and choose Start. Keyboard required.

Desktop keyboard. Fullscreen starts from your click where the browser supports it. Otherwise, Expand fills the available browser viewport; use Exit expanded view to return. On iPhone/iPad, browser bars may remain visible.

Saves stay on this device and origin. Reloading can restart an unsaved run. This arcade copy has separate browser storage from original hosted versions.
