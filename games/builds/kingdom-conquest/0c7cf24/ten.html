Controls and targeting

Right-click an enemy to attack or ground to move. Q / W / E / R aim toward the last cursor position. Cooldown numbers show seconds until ready; each ability lists its mana cost. B recalls to your fountain; F flashes toward the cursor. Space centers the camera, Y toggles follow, and Escape closes menus.

Mouse and keyboard are required for battle movement. Touch menus do not provide full phone gameplay.
