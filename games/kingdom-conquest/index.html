How to play

Choose Lincoln (gorilla) or Theodore (opossum), then right-click to move or attack the AI rival. Temporary demo controls: Q heavy strike, W ground slam, E move burst, R rally. Space centers the camera; scroll zooms. Mouse and keyboard are required for movement; touch can use menus and ability buttons.

Desktop mouse and keyboard. Fullscreen starts from your click where the browser supports it. Otherwise, Expand fills the available browser viewport; use Exit expanded view to return. On iPhone/iPad, browser bars may remain visible.

Saves stay on this device and origin. Reloading can restart an unsaved run. This arcade copy has separate browser storage from original hosted versions.
