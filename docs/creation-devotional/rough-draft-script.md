## Introduction

According to Pew Research Center's 2023–24 study, 61% of Christians in the U.S. say human evolution was guided or allowed by God or a higher power.

What percentage of Christians in the U.S. accept an old-Earth view, where the Earth is millions and millions of years old? And how many don't believe dinosaurs coexisted with humans?

My purpose for this devotional today is to help you think about this secondary issue. And I want to emphasize how important secondary issues are.

This issue, some might say, is an issue of scriptural authority. Are you going to take man's word, or are you going to take God's Word on how old the Earth is?

People have claimed in recent centuries that the Bible does not claim to tell us the age of the Earth. But I will be arguing today that the Bible does teach that the Earth is no older than 10,000 years old.

And there is an importance to this issue. And we'll go over it in this devotional today.

## Evolution

Evolution is an observable scientific fact. It's as plain as gravity.

It's how we get all the different dog breeds. It's how bacteria develop resistance to antibiotics. It's how insects develop resistance to pesticides.

Now, that may surprise you that I'm saying that. For those of you who know me, you might be very surprised at me saying this right now.

But let me tell you this. Did you know I was catholic?

Yeah, I hope all of us are catholic.

What does it mean in the Apostles' Creed when we all say together on Sundays, “I believe in the holy catholic Church”? Are we talking about the little church in Rome there? Are we talking about Vatican City?

No. We're talking about the universal church.

Words carry meaning. And terminology can be twisted throughout the years.

And so, first of all, I just want to share with you these six different categories of evolution:

1. Cosmic evolution.
2. Chemical evolution.
3. Stellar and planetary evolution.
4. Organic evolution, or abiogenesis—the origin of life.
5. Macroevolution.
6. Microevolution.

So we have these six different types of evolution. And only one of them, the last one, microevolution, has actual observable science behind it.

And I know the response: “Oh, but we observe stars forming and stuff.” No. We observe stars blowing up, and new stars or whatever, and stuff coming together. And you're going to tell me, “Oh, but it just takes so long.”

No, listen. We've been observing this for what, 3,000 years max? That is not enough time to witness stellar evolution on the scale of what they're talking about. And we've only been properly recording stellar history for the last maybe 100 or 200 years.

There's only one form of evolution that's properly documented, observable, repeatable, and seen in a lab. And that is microevolution. It's the only one.

So today I'm going to be referring to macroevolution as Darwinian evolution. And I'll be referring to microevolution as observable evolution, or I can also call it scientific evolution.

Because let me be clear: these five other definitions of evolution that exist, these five other types of evolution, they are not scientific. They are anything but. They are religious, they are faith-based, and they are ridiculous. And they're just dumb theories.

Theory is too strong of a word to use for all these different things. They wouldn't hold up in a court of law by any means. By any means, they would not hold up in a court of law.
