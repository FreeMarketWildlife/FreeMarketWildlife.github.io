# Creation devotional rough draft script

## Introduction

61% of Christians in the U.S. today think that we're distant cousins with apes—with the gorillas behind the bars in the zoo.

Now, Christians will often emphasize that whether the creation story in Genesis is literal or figurative is a secondary issue. But I'm here to tell you: secondary does not mean unimportant.

The primary issues in Christianity have to do with salvation. Is Jesus the Son of God? Is He fully God and fully man? Did He die for our sins and rise from the dead? Are we saved by grace through faith? The Trinity—one God, three persons.

Creationism is indeed a secondary issue. When you die and enter God's presence, He will not be asking you how old the Earth is. He's going to be asking, “What did you do with Jesus? Did you share the gospel? Did you follow My commands?”

God never commands you to believe that the Earth is 6,000 years old. Let me make that very plain. He also never explicitly commands you to believe that He is three persons in one being.

But here's what I'll say: the Scriptures are infallible. In the very first chapter of the Bible, God tests whether you have childlike faith in His Word.

Will we take God's Word, or will we take man's word?

Man's word has preached for over 200 years that the Earth came about by natural processes over billions of years. I will argue today that God's Word has preached for thousands of years, since Genesis was written by Moses, that the Earth is no older than 10,000 years old.

## Evolution

Evolution is an observable scientific fact. It's as plain as gravity.

It's how we get all the different dog breeds. It's how bacteria develop resistance to antibiotics and insects develop resistance to pesticides.

For those of you who know me, you might be very surprised to hear me say that.

But let me tell you this. Did you know I was catholic?

Yeah. I hope all of us are catholic.

When we say the Apostles' Creed together on Sundays, what do we mean by “the holy catholic Church”? Are we talking about the little church in Rome? Vatican City?

No. We're talking about the universal church.

Words carry meaning. And terminology evolves. (Wink wink.)

So first, I want to share these six different categories of evolution:

1. Cosmic evolution.
2. Chemical evolution.
3. Stellar and planetary evolution.
4. Organic evolution, or abiogenesis—the origin of life.
5. Macroevolution.
6. Microevolution.

Only one of them, microevolution, is properly documented, observable, repeatable, and seen in a lab.

And I know the response: “But we observe stars forming.” We observe stars blowing up and material coming together. And you're going to tell me, “It just takes so long.”

No, listen. We've been observing this for what, 3,000 years max? That is not enough time to witness stellar evolution on the scale they're talking about. We've only been properly recording stellar history for maybe 100 or 200 years.

So today, I'll refer to macroevolution as Darwinian evolution, and microevolution as observable evolution, or scientific evolution.

Let me be clear: these five other types of evolution are not scientific. They are religious, faith-based, and ridiculous. They're dumb theories.

Theory is too strong a word. They wouldn't hold up in a court of law.
