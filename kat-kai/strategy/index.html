Kat & Kai

Strategy

Weekly release cadence

1long-form video every week
3–7Shorts each week · at least 3

Shorts come from the weekly long-form video first. If it produces fewer than three usable Shorts, create original Shorts to bring the week’s total to at least three. Aim for more when there are enough strong moments.

Find our audience

We’re still building an audience and learning what connects. Use a consistent release cadence to discover which stories, moments, and formats people enjoy. Learn from the response and improve the next release.

Shorts targets: at least 70% stayed to watch and 70% retention. Long-form targets: to be defined.

Plan the story before filming

Start with Japan and Kat & Kai’s everyday experiences. Work together on the video idea, title, and thumbnail concept before filming. Stay authentic: speak to the camera as if talking with a friend, sharing what you notice, learn, and experience.

Filming & editing reminders
  • Default to filming whoever is speaking. When no one is speaking, capture interesting surroundings and moments together.
  • Keep the subject clearly framed, with enough room around the face. Use a comfortable camera distance or a selfie stick.
  • When capturing a scene, aim for continuous clips of at least 60 seconds so the edit has context around the moment.
  • Caption Kai’s speech and Japanese speech.
  • Capture possible thumbnail frames while recording, or take photos.
