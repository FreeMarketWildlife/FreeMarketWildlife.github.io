THE WEEKLY RHYTHM

One story. A week of moments.

1 long-form + 6 Shorts
  1. Saturday▶Long-formThe full story
  2. Sunday↗Short 1To Saturday’s video
  3. Monday↗Short 2To Saturday’s video
  4. Tuesday↗Short 3To Saturday’s video
  5. Wednesday↗Short 4To Saturday’s video
  6. Thursday↗Short 5To Saturday’s video
  7. Friday↗Short 6To Saturday’s video

↖ Each Short points back to Saturday’s full video.

Turn moments into discovery

Cut Shorts from the long-form first. Each needs a satisfying moment of its own, plus a reason to watch the full story.

Add Saturday’s video as the Short’s Related Video (advanced feature access required).

Keep the week achievable

Aim for all six daily Shorts. Keep the minimum at three per week; create original Shorts if the long-form does not supply enough.

Shorts targets: 70%+ stayed to watch and 70%+ retention. Long-form targets remain to be defined.

We’re finding our audience.

Test which moments connect and whether viewers continue to the full video. Cross-format viewing isn’t automatic. Keep the schedule sustainable; posting time and timezone are still to be set.
