Road to 10,000 ✦

10,000 subscribers by New Year’s

Deadline: January 1, 2027, at midnight Pacific. Work through 1,000, 2,000, 3,000, and every thousand through 10,000.

Checking the latest subscriber total and required pace…

Achievability: not established yet. Treat 10,000 as a stretch target until sustained growth supports it. A deadline and milestones alone do not make a target realistic.

A SMART first step

Proposed trial, October 19–November 2: publish two long-form videos and four Shorts, using the Shorts to highlight the long-form stories. Review results on October 26 and November 2, then choose the next two-week plan and an evidence-based date for the first 1,000 subscribers.

Specific
Choose one repeatable story format and turn each long-form release into two Shorts.
Measurable
Track six published pieces, two reviews, subscriber totals, and each video’s public performance.
Achievable
Confirm the workload with Kat first. Reduce the number of releases if it cannot be completed without rushing; record the revised commitment in her notes.
Relevant
Learn which stories connect with viewers and build a repeatable path toward the subscriber milestones.
Time bound
Finish the trial and review by November 2, 2026. Reassess the New Year target after each two-week review.

This trial is a proposal, not an agreed production commitment or a promise of subscriber growth. YouTube’s sustainable upload guidance.
