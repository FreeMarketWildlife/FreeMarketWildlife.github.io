Choose access for the work

Use the narrowest role that covers the agreed tasks.

  • Analytics: Viewer (Limited); use Viewer when revenue is needed.
  • Editing or uploads: Editor (Limited); use Editor when revenue is needed.
  • Subtitles only: Subtitle Editor.
  • Choose Manager or Owner only when administration is part of the work.

Brand Account access is a separate model. Its primary owner may need to move to Studio channel permissions and reinvite collaborators.
