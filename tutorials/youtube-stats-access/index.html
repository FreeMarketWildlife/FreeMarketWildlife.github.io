Show all steps
  1. Open YouTube Studio on a computer. Check the channel name, then click Settings at the bottom left.
  2. Choose Permissions.
  3. Click Invite.
  4. Enter freemarketwildlife@gmail.com in Email.
  5. Open Access and choose Viewer (limited).
  6. Check the email and role. Click Done. If the Settings Save button becomes available, click it.
  7. Check Permissions for the email and invitation status. If it shows as pending, your invitation is sent. You’re finished.
Reference image: Viewer (limited)
Don’t see Permissions or Invite?

Check that you’re signed into the correct channel’s owner account. If YouTube shows Brand Account permission settings instead, follow YouTube’s channel-permissions instructions, or contact us before changing anything.

What does this access allow?

We can review channel details and analytics, including private or unlisted content. Viewer (limited) cannot edit your videos or manage channel permissions, and excludes revenue data. You never share your Google password or verification codes.

This invitation provides access in YouTube Studio. It does not connect your stats to our website automatically.

How do I remove access?

In YouTube Studio, go to Settings → Permissions. Find freemarketwildlife@gmail.com, open its role menu, and choose Remove access. Save if prompted.
